Poise style guide

Bound to the code, not written beside it. This page reads tokens.css and the lint report when it loads and measures every token in your browser. Change anything in Style and the values below change with it, exactly as every view does.

Rules

Binding. The lint enforces the ones a machine can check; review holds the rest.
  1. Only tokens.css holds raw valuesNo hex, rgb, px, ms, weights or line heights anywhere else. Enforced.
  2. Components read semantic tokens onlyNever a control (--ctl-*) or a primitive (--p-*). Enforced.
  3. Colour is a meaningNeeds you, running, done, failed, agent, special, route. Nothing is coloured for decoration.
  4. Nothing gets brighterSurfaces come from the dark stack; the primary action is the soft neutral, never white.
  5. Two weightsRegular and strong for the interface; the display weight belongs to the display face only.
  6. Mono means machineIDs, times, hashes, commands and counts. Never texture.
  7. Borders at rest, shadows in motionHover, drag, menus and dialogs lift. Resting things never cast a shadow.
  8. Every term has a plain twinUppercase label for the proper name, a lowercase gloss for everyone else.
  9. The route languageWork travels a line; stations are capsules in the route colour; filled means done.
  10. Motion is a settingOne Tactile spring. Reduced motion, or the Style switch, sets every duration to zero.
